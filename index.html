Quick answers.

Do I pay for delivery?

No. Delivery is free. You only pay for the items you buy.

Do I pay for everything at once?

No. You pay for each item when you buy it. Paid items go into your bag.

What is the threshold?

It’s the amount your bag needs to reach before it ships. You can see where your bag stands in the app.

Where is my bag delivered?

To the location you choose.
